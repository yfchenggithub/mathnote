\textbf{根号来自直线方向的分量长度}\par\medskip
\begin{tikzpicture}[font=\small,>=Stealth]

\coordinate (U) at (0,0);\coordinate (V) at (4,0);\coordinate (W) at (4,3);
\draw[CStatement,thick] (U)--(V)--(W)--cycle;
\draw (3.75,0)--(3.75,.25)--(4,.25);
\pic[draw,angle radius=10mm,angle eccentricity=1.4,"$\theta$"] {angle=V--U--W};
\node[below] at (2,0) {$|B|$};
\node[right] at (4,1.5) {$|A|$};
\node[above left,rotate=36.87] at (2,1.5) {$\sqrt{A^2+B^2}$};
\node[align=left,anchor=west] at (5.8,1.5) {$\displaystyle\cos\theta=\frac{|B|}{\sqrt{A^2+B^2}}$};

\end{tikzpicture}\par\medskip
图取 $A=3,B=-4$。直线的方向向量可取 $(B,-A)$；水平、竖直分量的长度分别为 $|B|,|A|$，斜边长由勾股定理得到。
